## 💡 What they really want to know

People with a non-CS degree often get this question. The interviewer is not judging the degree.

They want to know:
- Why you chose software.
- How you learned it.
- Whether you keep learning on your own.

A clear, positive story answers all three.

## 🏠 Real-life example

Think of someone who learned to **cook from scratch**.

First they studied something else. Then they joined a cooking class. Then they got a job in a busy restaurant kitchen. Now they cook full meals and still take evening classes.

- **The first subject** = your BSc Physics.
- **The cooking class** = the MERN course at Brototype.
- **The restaurant job** = your work at SkillKeepr.
- **The evening classes** = your MCA.

Each step makes sense. That is how your answer should feel.

## 🧩 How to structure your answer

1. **Start (Physics):** what you studied, and one thing you liked about it.
2. **Turning point:** what pulled you towards software. [FILL IN: your real reason.]
3. **Learning (Brototype):** the MERN course — MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js.
4. **First job (SkillKeepr):** your role and what you grew into.
5. **Now:** the MCA, your recent AI work, and where you want to go next.

## 🗣️ Sample answer

> "I did my BSc in Physics and finished in 2022. I enjoyed the problem-solving side of it — taking a big problem and breaking it into small steps. [FILL IN: what first got you interested in programming.]
>
> So I decided to move into software. I joined the full stack MERN course at Brototype in Kozhikode, where I learned MongoDB, Express, React and Node.js by building projects.
>
> In May 2023, I joined Haspaces Technology Solutions as a Software Engineer, working on SkillKeepr, a multi-tenant recruitment platform. I started with [FILL IN: your first tasks]. Over time I took on bigger backend work — like the Stripe auto-renewal flow, the backend for an AI job-description chatbot, and an ATS integration through unified.to. Recently I built an AI voice agent that calls candidates automatically.
>
> Alongside work, I'm finishing my MCA at Amity Online, to strengthen my computer science basics. So it's been a steady path, and I'm now ready for [FILL IN: the next step you want]."

## ✅ Do

- Present Physics as a strength: logic, maths, problem-solving.
- Show growth: from small tasks to owning features.
- Mention that you keep learning (MCA, AI work).
- Keep each step to one or two sentences.
- Be ready to name one real project from the Brototype course. [FILL IN]

## ❌ Don't

- Don't apologise for not having a CS degree.
- Don't say "Physics had no jobs" or anything negative.
- Don't list every month and every course.
- Don't claim CS skills you can't explain.
- Don't drift into personal life details.
