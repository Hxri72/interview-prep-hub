## 💡 What they really want to know

This is usually the first question. It sets the mood for the whole interview.

The interviewer wants to know:
- Who you are, in one short story.
- What you work on now.
- Whether your experience fits this job.

They also listen to **how** you speak: clear, calm and confident.

## 🏠 Real-life example

Think of a **movie trailer**.

A trailer doesn't show the whole movie. It shows the best scenes in about a minute. You leave wanting to watch more.

- The **trailer** = your 60-second answer.
- The **best scenes** = your 2–3 strongest projects.
- **Wanting to watch more** = the interviewer asking follow-up questions about the projects you chose.

## 🧩 How to structure your answer

1. **Present (15–20 sec):** your role, company, product, and years of experience.
2. **Highlights (25–30 sec):** 2–3 things you built, in one line each.
3. **Past (10 sec):** how you got into software.
4. **Future (10 sec):** why this role is the next step.

Some people start with the past. Starting with the present is often stronger, because it leads with your current skill level.

## 🗣️ Sample answer

> "I'm Hariprasad, a Full Stack Engineer with over three years of experience. Since May 2023, I've worked at Haspaces Technology Solutions on SkillKeepr. It's a multi-tenant recruitment platform, where each company gets its own space. I work with React and TypeScript on the frontend, and Node.js and MongoDB on the backend.
>
> A few things I've built: the Stripe auto-renewal part of our subscription billing, the backend for an AI chatbot that helps recruiters write job descriptions, and an ATS integration through unified.to. Most recently, I built an AI voice agent. It automatically calls eligible candidates, collects basic details, and the calls are then scored by AI. This saves recruiters a lot of screening time.
>
> I studied Physics first. Then I did a MERN stack course at Brototype and moved into software. I'm also finishing my MCA.
>
> Now I'm looking for a role where I can [FILL IN: what you want — e.g. own bigger backend and AI features, work at larger scale]. That's why this position at [FILL IN: company] interests me."

That is about 60–70 seconds when spoken at a calm pace.

## ✅ Do

- Practise it out loud until it sounds natural, not memorised.
- Choose highlights that match the job description.
- Mention numbers only if they are true. For the voice agent, the time saving is an **estimate**.
- Smile and keep a steady pace.
- Prepare a 30-second version too.

## ❌ Don't

- Don't read your resume line by line.
- Don't start with "I was born in…" or school details.
- Don't go over 90 seconds.
- Don't use claims you can't explain in follow-up questions.
- Don't say anything negative about your current company.

## 🔁 Follow-up questions

### You mentioned the voice agent. Can you tell me more?

Use your [voice agent story](topic:resume/voice-agent-microservice): Twilio for calls, Google speech-to-text and text-to-speech, Claude for the thinking, a phase-based state machine, recordings stored in S3, and AI scoring.

### Why did you move from Physics to software?

Keep it positive and short. See [your journey](topic:hr/my-journey).

### What does SkillKeepr do?

It's a recruitment SaaS. It has a portal for recruiters, one for candidates, and one for SkillKeepr staff. See [the SkillKeepr overview](topic:resume/skillkeepr-overview).

### Which part of your work are you most proud of?

Pick one story and answer with STAR. See [the STAR method](topic:hr/star-method).
