## 💡 What they really want to know

The interviewer (often HR) wants to check:
- Are your expectations within their budget?
- Have you done your research, or are you guessing?
- Will you be reasonable to negotiate with?

A calm, researched range answers all three.

:::warning[Keep your real numbers private]
This site is public. Write your real current salary and expected range only in your private notes, never on this page.
:::

## 🏠 Real-life example

Think of **selling your second-hand bike**.

You first check what similar bikes sell for online. Then you say, "Bikes like this sell for between X and Y. I'm looking for around X." You sound fair, and the buyer trusts you.

- **Checking prices online** = market research.
- **"Between X and Y"** = your salary range.
- **Sounding fair and calm** = being easy to negotiate with.

## 🧩 How to structure your answer

1. **Show you researched:** "Based on my research for this role and experience…"
2. **Give a range:** a lower and upper number.
3. **Stay flexible:** "I'm open to discussing the full package."

**CTC basics in India (simple version):**

| Part | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Fixed pay** | Your basic salary plus allowances. Paid every month. |
| **Variable pay / bonus** | Depends on your or the company's performance. Not guaranteed. |
| **Employer PF** | Provident Fund the company pays into. Part of CTC, but you don't get it monthly in hand. |
| **Gratuity, insurance, other benefits** | Part of CTC, not cash in hand each month. |
| **In-hand salary** | What reaches your bank after tax and deductions. |

So two offers with the same CTC can give very different in-hand pay. Always ask for the breakup.

**If they ask for your current salary:**

> "My current CTC is [FILL IN: private]. But I'd like the offer to be based on this role and what I can bring, which is why I mentioned that range."

## ✅ Do

- Research the range before every interview.
- Ask for the breakup: fixed, variable, benefits.
- Know your minimum number — privately.
- Stay calm and polite, even if their number is low.
- Ask when the salary is reviewed and how.

## ❌ Don't

- Don't guess a number without research.
- Don't lie about your current salary. It is often checked from payslips.
- Don't compare only CTC between offers.
- Don't accept or reject on the spot if you need time — ask for a day to think.
- Don't publish your real numbers on this site.

## 🔁 Follow-up questions

### Is your expected salary negotiable?

"Yes, within reason. I'd look at the full package and the growth in the role." Keep your private minimum in mind.

### Why do you expect this much?

Link it to research and your skills: three years of full stack work, multi-tenant SaaS, payments, integrations and AI features.

### Do you have other offers?

Be honest. If you have one, you can mention it calmly, without sharing exact numbers unless you want to. [FILL IN: your situation.]

### Our budget is lower. Would you still join?

Ask about the fixed part, the review cycle and other benefits before answering. If you need time, say so.
