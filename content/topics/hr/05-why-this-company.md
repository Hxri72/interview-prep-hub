## 💡 What they really want to know

The interviewer wants to see:
- That you know what the company does.
- That you are genuinely interested, not just applying everywhere.
- That you can help them with real skills.

Ten minutes of research makes this answer strong.

## 🏠 Real-life example

Think of choosing a **cricket club to join**.

A good player doesn't say "any club is fine". They say: "Your club plays in the state league. I'm a good opening batsman, and you need one. I want to play at a higher level."

- **Knowing the club plays in the state league** = researching the company.
- **"You need an opening batsman"** = their needs from the job description.
- **"I'm a good opening batsman"** = your matching skills.

## 🧩 How to structure your answer

1. **What they do (1 line):** show you researched them.
2. **Why it interests you (1–2 lines):** something specific — the product, the scale, the tech, the mission.
3. **How you fit (1–2 lines):** link their needs to your real experience.

Where to research:
- Their website and product pages.
- The job description — underline the skills they repeat.
- Their engineering blog, GitHub, or tech talks.
- Recent news, funding, or product launches.
- Employee reviews and LinkedIn posts from their engineers.

## ✅ Do

- Mention one specific, true detail about the company.
- Use words from their job description.
- Link to your real experience with an example.
- Show enthusiasm, but stay natural.
- Prepare a different version for each company.

## ❌ Don't

- Don't give a generic answer that fits any company.
- Don't say "I don't know much about you yet".
- Don't make the answer only about salary, location or work-from-home.
- Don't over-praise. It sounds fake.
- Don't claim experience with their tech stack if you don't have it.

## 🔁 Follow-up questions

### What do you know about our product?

Explain it in 2–3 simple sentences. If you used it, say what you liked. [FILL IN: per company.]

### Who do you think our competitors are?

Name one or two if you researched them. If not, say honestly that you focused on their product.

### Which of our values matters most to you?

Pick one from their website and connect it to a real example from your work. [FILL IN]

### What would you improve in our product?

Be humble: "From the outside, I noticed X. I'd want to understand the reasons first, but I'd be curious about Y." [FILL IN]
